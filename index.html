Impact goals

SkillProof aims to narrow the gap between skill training and employment. These are goals, not proven results.

  • Improve practical skill verification
  • Expand access to micro-jobs and apprenticeships
  • Help employers identify suitable candidates
  • Encourage evidence-based portfolios
  • Improve earning access for underserved youth
